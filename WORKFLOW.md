# Production, review, and publication workflow

This independent edition follows the transcript-grounded NPTEL/Stanford textbook method. The source recordings establish the topic and timing spine; the exposition independently develops mathematics, worked constructions, primary-source extensions, executable laboratories, and fully reasoned exercise solutions.

1. Inventory the exact supplied playlist using stable video identities. Preserve both upload positions and official lecture numbers. Retain the introduction and identify the out-of-order put–call-parity upload without manufacturing missing material.
2. Acquire best-available English caption evidence and record provenance/digests. Keep raw sources privately under ignored work/. The official course's linked English VTTs provided the main acquisition route. Acquisition is not an attestation of reading.
3. Read assigned source text sequentially and record source-specific topics and timed landmarks in public source-reading ledgers. Check visually dependent material where necessary; state the actual audiovisual boundary.
4. Establish book-wide notation, assumptions, dependencies, and subject-specific review criteria. Read representative earlier chapters fully. Complete one pilot through authorship, independent review, execution, rendering, and public-input rebuild before scaling production.
5. Develop each chapter in coverage, prose, mathematics, research, implementation, exercise, editorial, parity, and validation passes. Put definitions before use; derive consequential steps; interpret equations; compute printed values. Preserve source distinctions and label original constructions.
6. Obtain two independent scientific/editorial acceptances for each actual manuscript, metadata file, and laboratory. A reviewer never accepts their own authored chapter. Repair located findings and bind current acceptance to exact SHA-256 digests. Preserve review scope and unresolved limits.
7. Run chapter laboratories in fresh processes and check meaningful mathematical certificates. Audit full source coverage, notation, solved exercises, local links, timestamp bounds, and the staged public tree. Rebuild the reader from public authored inputs without private acquisition artifacts.
8. Validate mathematical typesetting and controls offline at desktop/phone widths. Wide formulas remain scrollable within the reading column while visible math scrollbars are hidden. Test code copy, disclosures, navigation, print expansion, and interactive model behavior.
9. Commit the public educational edition, push to the new repository, and deploy through checked GitHub Actions. Inspect the actual served release rather than inferring success from a push. Record publication verification separately from scientific review.

The chapter craft draws on the transcript-grounded-textbook and rigorous-explainer methods, with science-editor's claim standard and analyze-sources provenance discipline. Book-wide structure follows STEM textbook dependency and notation practices. The user's explicit authorization covers autonomous planning, completion, and publication for this edition.

## Public and local material

Public: editable chapters, generated reader, original figures and their generators, labs, local rendering assets and licenses, compact source metadata, sanitized review/validation records, rebuild tools, and dependency specifications. Private/ignored: raw captions and media, downloaded papers, acquisition logs, personal machine paths, scratch drafts, and local handoff material. A directory name alone does not establish publication rights.

Run the commands in README.md. Final release checks require the entire chapter/source scope and two current independent acceptances. The --allow-partial flag is for development only and must not be used as evidence that the full edition is complete.

## Evidence boundaries

Caption reading does not certify every visible board equation or every audible word. AI editorial review is not human expert certification or formal proof verification. Numerical checks establish their stated finite computations. A byte-identical rebuild establishes reproducibility, not financial correctness. Source timestamps locate related discussion without attributing every new derivation or example to the lecturer.

The postpublication [critical comparison](critical-comparison.md) fully reads selected earlier NPTEL and Stanford exemplars and rereads the current manuscripts before choosing repairs. New incremental review receipts preserve earlier full-review history and bind the revised content only after independent inspection. A comparison against selected exemplars does not establish a universal ranking of earlier publications.

The [mathematical companion](mathematical-repairs.html) is built from its public Markdown source and has a separate laboratory. The book specification declares its author and required inputs. Release validation requires two current independent non-author acceptances for those inputs, executes the companion lab, and reproduces its reader page without private files. Desktop/phone browser checks include its mathematics, navigation, worked-check disclosures, code copy, theme, and print controls.
