# Critical comparison with earlier NPTEL and Stanford notes

Revision date: 24 November 2026. Comparison parent: `6c94f56bd237b6e2d3417aa9f8e75597f264a7bc`.

The previous edition had complete topic coverage and substantial derivations, but several important consequences received only a warning, a formula, or a numerical observation. The strongest earlier chapters develop those consequences into explicit certificates: what the result controls, why it holds, how to check it, and where it fails. This revision applies that standard to the identified gaps. It also improves paragraph structure, exercise reasoning, terminology, and prerequisite references.

## Explanatory depth and style

The revisions place the reasoning beside the result it supports. Exercise questions and solutions were expanded where they now test the added mechanisms. Code contracts and numerical checks were updated to match the exposition. Dense paragraphs were split by intellectual move. Cramped word/number spacing and prerequisite labels were repaired. Source-caption corrections were moved out of the scientific argument into concise scope notes where appropriate, so the main prose teaches the mathematics rather than narrating manuscript production.

The strongest earlier chapters do not merely name a limitation. They demonstrate its consequence. This edition now follows that pattern more consistently: a beta target can leave a larger variance, a delivery switch can defeat a matched DV01, correct marginal laws can specify a wrong joint process, and a variance-preserving mapping can destroy a portfolio offset.
